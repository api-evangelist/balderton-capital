OSSInsight/Balderton Capital— unlocking the power of open-source With the rise of GenerativeAI, everyone is talking about the evolution of open-source software again — and for good reason. At Balderton Capital, we’ve long been excited about the potential for open-source software, with investments like MySQL, Talend, GitGuardian and Ory in more recent years. Following our recent investment in Deepset ($30m raised to help deploy LLMs), we were keen to share our approach to sourcing great open-source companies, and our further deployment towards software companies.
