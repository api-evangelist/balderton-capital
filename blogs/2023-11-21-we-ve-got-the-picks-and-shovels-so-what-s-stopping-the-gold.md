We’ve got the picks and shovels — so what’s stopping the gold miners? It’s becoming increasingly clear that there’s a massive chasm between the amount being invested into AI infrastructure and foundation models vs the amount of end-customer value being created on the application layer. We try out dozens of new AI tools every month but I can count the number of tools I’m actually a WAU of on one hand.
